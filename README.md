# Co-op Transfers Deadline Tracker

Prototype deadline tracker for NYC co-op transfer applications under Local Law 58 of 2026 (Int. 1120-B).

**All data is fictional.** Buildings, units, and applicants are generated for demonstration only.

## What this is

A simple operational view that keeps every open transfer file sorted by days remaining on the 15-day acknowledgment clock or the 45-day decision clock. It flags incomplete packages, tracks ownership, and shows illustrative fine exposure.

## Why it matters

Local Law 58 requires managing agents to acknowledge applications within 15 days (email + registered mail) and issue a decision within 45 days. Missing either deadline can result in HPD fines. This tracker makes the clocks visible and prioritizes work by urgency.
