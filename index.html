I built this tracker after reading Local Law 58 of 2026, which requires managing agents to acknowledge co-op transfer applications within 15 days and issue a decision within 45 days. It keeps every open file sorted by days remaining, flags incomplete packages, and shows fine exposure so deadlines do not get missed as volume grows.
Disclaimer: Fictional buildings, units, and applicants only. Deadlines follow the 15-day acknowledgment and 45-day decision clocks under NYC Local Law 58. Fine exposure is illustrative ($1,000 per overdue file). Verify against official Administrative Code text.
